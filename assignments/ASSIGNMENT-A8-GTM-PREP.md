# Assignment A8 – Go-To-Market Preparation

**Status:** Not Started  
**Owner:** Solo Dev (acting PMM/GTM lead)  
**Branch:** `release/1.0.0`

## Objective
Prepare all external-facing launch materials, pricing collateral, and communication plans required to successfully introduce Uveddi 1.0.0 to the market. Align messaging with product capabilities and ensure launch channels are coordinated.

## Deliverables
1. **Launch Messaging Framework**
   - Positioning statement, key value pillars, differentiators, and proof points (`docs/gtm/messaging-framework.md`).
2. **Pricing & Packaging Overview**
   - Pricing tiers, feature matrices, licensing terms (`docs/gtm/pricing-packaging.md`).
3. **Launch Announcement Assets**
   - Blog post draft, email announcement, social media copy, and press release outline.
4. **Website / Landing Page Updates**
   - Updated copy, feature lists, CTA buttons, and visual assets (wireframes or mockups).
5. **Launch Runbook**
   - Detailed schedule for announcements, responsible parties, and contingency plan (`docs/gtm/launch-runbook.md`).

## Tasks
1. **Audience & Persona Alignment**
   - Define primary buyer personas and user segments; map needs to product features.
2. **Messaging & Positioning**
   - Craft core story (problem → solution → proof) validated against competitive landscape.
   - Compile proof points from QA results, calibration metrics, and customer testimonials (if available).
3. **Pricing Strategy**
   - Finalize pricing tiers, seat limits, and feature entitlements.
   - Document licensing model (per-seat, per-analysis, hybrid) and discount policies.
4. **Content Creation**
   - Draft announcement blog, release email, and social posts with consistent messaging.
   - Coordinate visuals/screenshots from docs/quickstart to include.
5. **Web & Asset Updates**
   - Outline required changes to website or documentation portal.
   - Prepare asset checklist (icons, screenshots, diagrams) and storage paths.
6. **Launch Planning**
   - Build launch calendar with key dates/times, internal dry-run, and approval gates.
   - Prepare FAQ for sales/support teams plus escalation plan for launch-day issues.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Messaging framework approved and stored under version control.
- Pricing/packaging document aligns with product capabilities and license enforcement plan.
- Announcement content ready for scheduling with final copy & asset links.
- Website change list delivered with copy updates and owner assignments.
- Launch runbook includes timeline, channel checklist, and risk mitigation steps.

## Verification Steps
1. Reviewer checks messaging framework for clarity and alignment with product differentiators.
2. Reviewer validates pricing document covers tiers, features, and licensing notes.
3. Reviewer confirms announcement drafts are ready (markdown or docs) with asset placeholders.
4. Reviewer inspects launch runbook to ensure responsibilities and contact info are clear.
5. Tracker updated with GTM readiness status; risk register updated if launch risks mitigated.

## Dependencies & Notes
- Requires finalized docs/enablement materials (Assignment A7) and packaging artifacts (Assignment A6).
- Coordinate with support readiness (Assignment A9) to align messaging about service levels.
- Consider previewing messaging with design partners or beta users for feedback.
